## 🔄 How Withinly Works

### 1. Reflect

Short, thoughtful questions reveal how you connect, express care, stay present, and meet yourself.  
Takes about five minutes — no labels, no judgment.

### 2. See Yourself Clearly

Your answers become a short personal reflection that explains your behaviors:  
why you react in conflict, how you show love, and what helps you feel safe.  
Specific, recognizable — not generic.

### 3. Explore Real Moments

Ask about what’s happening now:  
“Why did I shut down in that talk?” • “How do I ask for what I need?”  
It’s like talking to a wise friend who remembers.

### 4. Connect When Ready

Sync with your partner to see how your patterns meet.  
You both get personalized insights; only shared insights are visible.
